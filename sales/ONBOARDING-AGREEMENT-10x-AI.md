# SALES REPRESENTATIVE ONBOARDING AGREEMENT

**BETWEEN:** 10x AI ("Company")  
**AND:** ________________________________ ("Representative")

## 1. Role and Responsibilities

Representative is engaged to perform sales duties for 10x AI, including:

- Engaging and following up with warm leads in the 10x AI Telegram community
- Converting demo traders into funded live traders (FTDs)
- Closing re-deposits from existing funded traders
- Using Company-provided scripts, objection-handling frameworks, and sequences
- Reporting daily numbers: contacts made, closes, deposits secured
- Participating in team check-ins and performance reviews
- Representing 10x AI professionally in all customer interactions

## 2. Schedule and Coverage

- **Weekday Shift length:** 10 hours per day (Monday–Friday)
- **Weekend Shifts:** Compulsory. 5 hours per day (Saturday & Sunday). May occasionally extend based on traffic volume.
- **Coverage:** Representative is expected to be available and responsive during their shift hours
- **Handoff:** Representative will brief the incoming shift Representative on active leads and pending follow-ups
- **Shift schedules** will be communicated weekly

### Check-In / Check-Out Protocol

- **Check-In:** At the start of every shift, Representative must post a check-in notice in the employee group chat confirming they are online and starting their shift
- **Check-Out:** At the end of every shift, Representative must post a check-out notice in the employee group chat including:
  - Total FTDs (first-time deposits) closed during the shift
  - Number of re-deposits closed during the shift
  - Number of pending potential closes (leads still in discussion)
- Failure to check in/out consistently may affect performance evaluation

## 3. Compensation

### Base Salary
- **Day Shift:** ₦100,000 per month
- **Night Shift:** ₦120,000 per month

### Performance Bonus
- **₦30,000 per month** if weekly KPI targets are met for at least 3 out of 4 weeks in the month
- KPIs are set weekly and communicated in advance

### Weekly KPI Contest
- The top-performing Representative each week (based on published KPI metrics) wins a separate cash prize
- Prize amount communicated weekly

### Data Allowance
- **₦10,000 per month** data allowance to cover internet costs during shifts

### Payment
- Salary and bonuses are paid monthly
- Payment method and date will be communicated

## 4. Performance Expectations (KPIs)

Representative agrees to meet weekly KPI targets which may include:

- Number of leads contacted
- First-time deposits (FTDs) closed
- Total deposit volume secured
- Re-deposits from existing funded traders
- Response time and engagement metrics

Targets are set weekly and reviewed each week. Consistent underperformance (below 50% of target for 3 consecutive weeks) may result in review or termination.

## 5. Tools and Access

Company will provide Representative with:

- Access to 10x AI Telegram community and channels
- Sales scripts, objection handlers, and follow-up sequences
- CRM or tracking sheet for lead management
- Brand assets and approved messaging

Representative agrees to:

- Use Company tools and platforms exclusively for Company business
- Not share access credentials
- Report any platform issues promptly

## 6. Independent Contractor Status

Representative is engaged as an **independent contractor**, not an employee. Representative is responsible for:

- Their own tax obligations
- Their own work equipment (phone, internet)
- Their own work environment

## 7. Confidentiality

Representative has separately signed the 10x AI Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement, which is incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with **14 days' written notice**.

Company may terminate immediately without notice for:

- Breach of confidentiality or NDA
- Misrepresentation or fraud (falsifying numbers, fabricating deposits)
- Misconduct toward customers or community members
- Sharing Company data or access with unauthorized parties
- Any action that damages the Company's reputation or business

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
