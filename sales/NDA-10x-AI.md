# NON-DISCLOSURE AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

**BETWEEN:** 10x AI ("Company")  
**AND:** ________________________________ ("Representative")  
**DATE:** ____________________

## 1. Purpose

Representative will have access to confidential and proprietary information belonging to 10x AI, including but not limited to business methods, trading strategies, marketing funnels, customer data, scripts, software architecture, and financial data. This Agreement protects that information.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" includes all non-public information disclosed by the Company to the Representative, whether written, oral, or observed, including:

- Customer and lead data (names, contact details, trading activity, deposit history)
- Sales scripts, objection-handling frameworks, and follow-up sequences
- Bot architecture, trading algorithms, signal strategies, and drain/demo mechanics
- Revenue models, pricing structures, affiliate arrangements
- Channel and community management strategies
- Marketing funnel design, ad creative, landing page structure
- All internal communications, dashboards, and reporting tools

## 3. Obligations

Representative agrees to:

a) **Not disclose** any Confidential Information to any third party, during or after engagement, without prior written consent  
b) **Not use** Confidential Information for any purpose other than performing duties for the Company  
c) **Not copy, reproduce, or remove** any Company data, documents, or systems  
d) **Not share** access credentials or allow unauthorized access to Company platforms  
e) **Immediately report** any unauthorized disclosure or security breach  
f) **Return or destroy** all Confidential Information upon termination or at the Company's request

## 4. Non-Compete

During the engagement and for a period of **12 months** following termination, Representative shall not:

a) Work for or advise any competing trading signal, automated trading, or IQ Option bot business  
b) Solicit any 10x AI customers, leads, or community members for any competing offering  
c) Use 10x AI's methods, scripts, or strategies to build or operate a competing business

## 5. Duration

This Agreement is effective from the date above and survives termination of the Representative's engagement indefinitely, except for the Non-Compete clause (Section 4) which expires 12 months after termination.

## 6. Breach and Remedies

Representative acknowledges that breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to the Company. The Company is entitled to:

a) Immediate termination of engagement  
b) Injunctive relief to prevent further disclosure  
c) Monetary damages for losses suffered  
d) Recovery of legal costs incurred in enforcement

## 7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
