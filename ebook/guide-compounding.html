1. What compounding actually is

Compounding means your profits stay in the account and the next trade is sized off the new, larger balance. You never withdraw the winnings mid-cycle, and you never reset your stake back to the original amount.

On IQ Option binary options this works because each trade has a fixed payout. When you stake a percentage of your balance and win, the profit is a percentage of the current balance — so a 10% account week is always 10% of whatever the account has grown to.

THE COMPOUND FORMULA

Balance after N weeks = Start × (1 + weekly return)N

$100 at +10%/week → after 8 weeks: 100 × 1.18 = $214
$100 at +15%/week → after 8 weeks: 100 × 1.158 = $306
$1,000 at +10%/week → after 8 weeks: 1,000 × 1.18 = $2,144

The engine (10x AI) finds the direction. The compounding method decides how much to risk and what to do with the result. You control the growth; the engine controls the entries.

2. The mechanics on IQ Option

You trade OTC pairs (EURUSD-OTC, GBPUSD-OTC, and the others) on short timeframes. Each trade is a binary option: you predict whether the price ends above (Higher/BUY) or below (Lower/SELL) the entry when the timer expires.

Payouts

OTC pairs pay roughly 86% on a winning trade. If you stake $100 and win, you get your $100 back plus $86 profit. If you lose, you lose the $100.

What that means for compounding

A winning trade at 86% payout grows the account by 0.86 × stake%. A losing trade shrinks it by the stake%. So the two numbers that decide everything are:

  • Your win rate — how often the direction is right (10x AI's job)
  • Your stake size — the % of balance per trade (your job)

WHY STAKE SIZE MATTERS MORE THAN WIN RATE

At 86% payout and a 60% win rate, a 2% stake nets ≈ +0.23% per trade on average. A 10% stake with the same win rate nets ≈ +1.16% — but a single losing streak of 4 trades costs −34% of the balance. Small stakes survive the streaks; large stakes die before the streak ends.

3. The one rule everything hangs on

NEVER RISK MORE THAN YOUR RULE ALLOWS

Stake = a fixed percentage of the current live balance, recalculated after every trade. Not a fixed dollar amount. Not "what I feel like." A percentage.

6. The mistakes that kill compounding

  • Raising the stake after a win. The stake rises with the balance, never because you feel hot. Doubling the % after two wins is how a −8% day becomes a −30% day.
  • Recovering with size. A lost trade is one trade. The engine's recovery rounds are designed and capped — your manual "get it back now" stake is not.
  • Withdrawing mid-cycle. The moment you pull profit out, the base stops compounding. Withdrawals belong at the end of the cycle.
  • Trading every signal. More trades is not more profit. The daily loss stop protects you from yourself.
  • Quitting after one bad week. A −8% week is a dip, not a failure. The compounding method is measured in months.
"The account that survives the bad weeks is the account that compounds."

8. Quick answers

Can I compound with $50?

Yes. 2% of $50 is a $1 stake — tiny but real. The compounding table is the same; the absolute numbers just take longer to look impressive. Protect the account and the percentages do the work.

What if I have a red week?

You stop at the daily loss stop, log it, and continue next week from the new balance. The stake recalibrates automatically because it is a percentage.

Autopilot or Private Trader?

Autopilot compounds while you are away — best for big capital consistency. Private Trader builds the discipline — best for small capital learning the method. Most serious compounders run both.

Is this guaranteed?

No. Nothing is guaranteed, and no system is perfect. This guide reduces the risk of losing your base and gives growth a fair chance — it does not remove risk from trading.
